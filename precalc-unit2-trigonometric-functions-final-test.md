### Bonus

**B1.** On the unit circle, any point P(θ) has coordinates (cos θ, sin θ).
The distance from the origin to P is the radius, which equals 1.
By the distance formula: √((cos θ)² + (sin θ)²) = 1
Squaring both sides: cos²θ + sin²θ = 1

**B2.**
   a) After t = 2 seconds, angle = ωt = 3(2) = 6 radians
      x = 6 cos(6) ≈ 5.76
      y = 6 sin(6) ≈ -1.68
      Coordinates: approximately (5.76, -1.68)

   b) Distance = arc length = rθ = 6(6) = 36 units

**B3.** Let α = arctan(3/4) and β = arccos(5/13)
For α: tan α = 3/4, so in right triangle: opp = 3, adj = 4, hyp = 5
Therefore: sin α = 3/5, cos α = 4/5

For β: cos β = 5/13, so in right triangle: adj = 5, hyp = 13, opp = 12
Therefore: sin β = 12/13, cos β = 5/13

Using sum formula (from Unit 3):
sin(α + β) = sin α cos β + cos α sin β
           = (3/5)(5/13) + (4/5)(12/13)
           = 15/65 + 48/65
           = 63/65
