## Part 3: Statistical Sampling (15 points)

**11.** (5 points) Explain the difference between a population and a sample. Give an example of each.

**12.** (5 points) A school has 600 students. In a random sample of 50 students, 12 play a musical instrument. Based on this sample, how many students in the entire school would you estimate play a musical instrument? Show your work.

**13.** (5 points) Is this sampling method biased or unbiased? Explain your reasoning.
"To find out favorite TV shows of teenagers, you survey teenagers at a sporting event."

---

## Part 4: Measures of Center and Variability (30 points)

**14.** (6 points) Find the mean and median of this data set: 12, 15, 18, 20, 25

**15.** (6 points) Find the range of this data set: 34, 28, 45, 31, 52, 38

**16.** (8 points) For the data set: 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40
   a) Find the median (Q2)
   b) Find Q1
   c) Find Q3
   d) Find the IQR

**17.** (10 points) Two classes took the same test. Their scores are summarized below:
```
Class A: Mean = 78, Range = 40
Class B: Mean = 82, Range = 20
```
a) Which class had a higher average score?
b) Which class had more consistent scores? Explain.
c) If you could only know one piece of information about a class's performance, would you prefer to know the mean or the range? Explain your reasoning.

**Part 3: Statistical Sampling**
11. Population is the entire group; sample is a subset. Example: Population = all US adults; Sample = 1000 randomly selected adults
12. 12/50 = 24%; 0.24 × 600 = 144 students
13. Biased; teenagers at sporting events may not represent all teenagers' TV preferences

**Part 4: Measures of Center and Variability**
14. Mean = 18; Median = 18
15. Range = 24
16. a) Q2 = 25; b) Q1 = 15; c) Q3 = 35; d) IQR = 20
17. a) Class B (82 vs 78)
    b) Class B more consistent (smaller range)
    c) Answers vary; could argue mean shows typical performance, or range shows consistency

**Bonus**
18. No, not the same. $100 item: After 50% markup = $150. After 50% discount = $75. Final price ($75) is less than original ($100). This is because the 50% discount is calculated on the marked-up price ($150), not the original.
