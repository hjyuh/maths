## Answer Key (For Teacher Use)

**Part 1: Statistical Questions**
1. a) NO - There is one specific answer (the actual number of students)
   b) YES - Answers will vary from student to student
2. Example: "What are the favorite foods of students in 6th grade?"
   This is statistical because different students will have different favorite foods (variability in answers)

**Part 2: Measures of Center**
3. Mean = 11.3 (rounded from 79/7)
   Work: (8 + 12 + 10 + 15 + 9 + 14 + 11) ÷ 7 = 79 ÷ 7 ≈ 11.3
4. Median = 11
   Ordered: 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15
   Middle value: 11
5. No mode (all values appear once)
6. Mean or median would both work well (no outliers present). Either answer is acceptable with proper justification.
   The mean includes all data values. The median represents the middle value.

**Part 3: Measures of Variability**
7. Range = 25
   Maximum (30) - Minimum (5) = 25
8. IQR = 15
   Ordered: 5, 8, 10, 12, 15, 18, 20, 25, 30
   Median = 15
   Q1 = 9 (median of 5, 8, 10, 12)
   Q3 = 24 (median of 18, 20, 25, 30)
   IQR = Q3 - Q1 = 24 - 9 = 15
9. MAD = 6.4
   Mean = 15.9 (rounded from 143/9)
   Distances: |5-15.9|=10.9, |8-15.9|=7.9, |10-15.9|=5.9, |12-15.9|=3.9,
              |15-15.9|=0.9, |18-15.9|=2.1, |20-15.9|=4.1, |25-15.9|=9.1, |30-15.9|=14.1
   Sum of distances = 58.9
   MAD = 58.9 ÷ 9 ≈ 6.5 (accept 6.4-6.6 depending on rounding)

**Bonus**
