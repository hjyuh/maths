# Grade 7 Unit 1: Practice Problems

## Instructions
- Show all work for each problem
- Include units in your answers where appropriate
- Check your work using the answer key at the end
- If you get a problem wrong, review the notes and try again

---

## Section 1: Understanding Ratios (8 problems)

**1.** Express the ratio 12:18 in simplest form.

**2.** In a fruit basket, there are 8 apples and 12 oranges. What is the ratio of apples to oranges?

**3.** A paint mixture uses 3 parts blue to 5 parts yellow. If you use 15 parts blue, how many parts yellow do you need?

**4.** The ratio of students to teachers at a school is 18:1. If there are 450 students, how many teachers are there?

**5.** Write three equivalent ratios for 4:7.

**6.** A recipe calls for 2 cups of sugar to 5 cups of flour. What is the ratio of sugar to flour in simplest form?

**7.** In a class of 30 students, the ratio of boys to girls is 3:2. How many boys and how many girls are in the class?

**8.** Are the ratios 6:9 and 10:15 equivalent? Explain your reasoning.

---

## Section 2: Rates and Unit Rates (10 problems)

**9.** Find the unit rate: 240 miles in 4 hours.

**10.** A package of 6 bottles of water costs $3.90. What is the unit price per bottle?

**11.** Jennifer types 450 words in 9 minutes. What is her typing rate in words per minute?

**12.** Which is the better buy: 3 pounds of apples for $4.50 or 5 pounds for $7.00?

**13.** A car travels 315 miles on 15 gallons of gas. How many miles per gallon does the car get?

**14.** Marcus reads 120 pages in 3 hours. At this rate, how many pages can he read in 7 hours?

**15.** A store sells 4 notebooks for $6.60 or 7 notebooks for $11.20. Which is the better deal?

**16.** Water flows from a faucet at 2.5 gallons per minute. How much water flows in 12 minutes?

**17.** If 8 ounces of juice cost $1.60, how much would 20 ounces cost?

**18.** A printer prints 18 pages in 3 minutes. How long will it take to print 84 pages?

## Section 3: Identifying Proportional Relationships (10 problems)

**19.** Is the relationship in this table proportional? Explain.
```
x | y
--|--
2 | 8
4 | 16
6 | 24
```

**20.** Is the relationship in this table proportional? Explain.
```
x | y
--|--
1 | 7
3 | 11
5 | 15
```

**21.** Find the constant of proportionality for the table in problem 19.

**22.** Does the equation y = 4x + 3 represent a proportional relationship? Why or why not?

**23.** Does the equation y = 7x represent a proportional relationship? What is the constant of proportionality?

**24.** A graph passes through the points (0,0), (2,6), and (4,12). Is this a proportional relationship?

**25.** Which of these graphs represents a proportional relationship?
- Graph A: A straight line passing through (1,2) and (3,6)
- Graph B: A straight line passing through (0,3) and (2,7)

**26.** Complete the table for a proportional relationship with constant k = 5:
```
x | y
--|--
0 | ?
3 | ?
? | 25
8 | ?
```

**27.** If y is proportional to x and y = 20 when x = 4, what is y when x = 9?

**28.** The cost c is proportional to the number of items n. If 6 items cost $24, find the constant of proportionality.

**33.** Write an equation for: y is proportional to x with a constant of proportionality of 8.

```
Hours (h) | Earnings ($e)
```

## Section 5: Solving Proportions (10 problems)

**37.** Solve for x: x/6 = 5/3

**38.** Solve for n: 4/7 = n/21

**39.** Solve for y: 9/y = 3/5

**40.** Solve for a: 2/5 = 8/a

**41.** If 5 pencils cost $2.25, how much do 12 pencils cost?

**42.** A map has a scale of 1 inch = 50 miles. If two cities are 3.5 inches apart on the map, what is the actual distance?

**43.** Solve for m: m/12 = 7/4

**44.** A recipe for 6 servings uses 2 cups of rice. How much rice is needed for 15 servings?

**45.** Solve for p: 5/8 = 15/p

**46.** If a car travels 180 miles in 3 hours, how far will it travel in 5 hours at the same speed?

## Section 8: Multi-Step Problems and Applications (14 problems)

**67.** A model airplane has a scale of 1:48. If the model's wingspan is 10 inches, what is the actual wingspan in feet?

**68.** A 5-pound bag of flour costs $3.75. A 12-pound bag costs $8.40. Which is the better buy per pound?

**69.** Circle A has a radius of 5 cm. Circle B has a diameter of 12 cm. Find the difference in their circumferences.

**70.** A car travels 280 miles on 14 gallons of gas. How many gallons are needed to travel 500 miles?

**71.** The ratio of length to width in a rectangle is 5:3. If the length is 35 cm, what is the width?

**72.** A circular flower bed has a diameter of 8 feet. You want to put a border around it that costs $4 per foot. What will the border cost?

**73.** Three numbers are in the ratio 2:3:5. If the smallest number is 18, what are the other two numbers?

**74.** A circular pizza with a diameter of 12 inches costs $10. A circular pizza with a diameter of 16 inches costs $15. Which pizza is the better value per square inch?

**75.** On a map, 2 inches represents 75 miles. How many inches would represent 262.5 miles?

**76.** The ratio of boys to girls in a club is 4:5. If there are 36 students total, how many are girls?

**77.** A circular running track has a circumference of 400 meters. What is the radius of the track?

**78.** If 3 workers can complete a job in 8 hours, how long would it take 6 workers working at the same rate?

**79.** A wheel with a radius of 35 cm makes 100 complete rotations. What distance has been traveled?

**80.** The ratio of red marbles to blue marbles in a bag is 3:7. If there are 60 marbles total, how many are red?
