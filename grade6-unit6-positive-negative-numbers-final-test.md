## Part 1: Understanding Integers and Absolute Value (20 points)

**1.** (3 points) Write an integer to represent each situation:
   a) 20 feet below sea level
   b) A gain of $15

**2.** (4 points) Find the absolute value:
   a) |-12|
   b) |7|

**3.** (4 points) Compare using <, >, or =:
   a) -5 ___ -2
   b) |-8| ___ |8|

**4.** (4 points) Order these numbers from least to greatest: 3, -7, 0, -2, 5

**5.** (5 points) What is the opposite of -9? Explain what "opposite" means in your own words.
