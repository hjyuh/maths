## Part 1: Understanding and Writing Ratios (20 points)

**1.** (4 points) A fruit bowl contains 9 bananas and 6 apples. Write the ratio of bananas to apples in three different ways.

**2.** (4 points) In a parking lot, there are 15 motorcycles and 25 cars. Write the ratio of motorcycles to total vehicles in simplest form.

**3.** (6 points) A paint mixture contains red, blue, and yellow paint in a ratio of 3:2:1.
   a) What is the ratio of red paint to blue paint?
   b) What is the ratio of yellow paint to the total mixture?
   c) If the mixture contains 18 cups total, how many cups of red paint are there?

**4.** (6 points) In a classroom of 32 students, the ratio of boys to girls is 3:5. How many boys and how many girls are in the class? Show your work using a diagram or calculation.

## Answer Key (For Teacher Use)

**Part 1: Understanding and Writing Ratios**
1. 9 to 6, 9:6, 9/6 (or simplified: 3 to 2, 3:2, 3/2)
2. 15:40 = 3:8
3. a) 3:2
   b) 1:6 or 1/6
   c) 9 cups of red paint
4. Boys: 12, Girls: 20
   (Total parts = 3+5 = 8; Each part = 32÷8 = 4; Boys = 3×4 = 12; Girls = 5×4 = 20)

**Part 2: Equivalent Ratios**
5. No. 8:12 = 2:3; 10:15 = 2:3. Actually, YES they are equivalent!
   (Both simplify to 2:3)
6. 2:3
7. a) 24
   b) 7
   c) 9
8. 2:7, 4:14, 6:21, 8:28, 10:35 (or any five correct equivalent ratios)

**Part 3: Using Ratio Tables**
9.
| Flour (cups) | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 | 15 |
|--------------|---|---|---|-----|-----|
| Eggs         | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8  | 10 |

10. a) 250 miles
    b) 6 hours
11. $35

**Part 4: Part-to-Whole Ratios**
12. a) 30 red marbles
    b) 18 blue marbles
    (Total parts = 5+3 = 8; Each part = 48÷8 = 6; Red = 5×6 = 30; Blue = 3×6 = 18)
13. a) Sibling 1: $2,400; Sibling 2: $3,600; Sibling 3: $6,000
    b) 2/10 = 1/5
    (Total parts = 2+3+5 = 10; Each part = $12,000÷10 = $1,200)

**Part 5: Real-World Applications**
14. 187.5 miles
    (Set up ratio: 2:75 = 5:x; x = (5×75)÷2 = 187.5)
15. Store A: $0.75 per bottle; Store B: $0.70 per bottle
    Store B has the better price
16. a) 240 copies
    b) 15 minutes

**Part 6: Challenge Problem**
17. Width = 12 cm, Length = 30 cm
    (Ratio 2:5 means width = 2x and length = 5x
    Perimeter = 2(2x + 5x) = 2(7x) = 14x = 84
    x = 6, so width = 12 cm and length = 30 cm)

**Bonus**
18. Orange juice: 24 oz, Pineapple juice: 18 oz, Soda: 30 oz
    (Total parts = 4+3+5 = 12; Each part = 72÷12 = 6 oz)
