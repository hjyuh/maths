## Extra Credit (2 points)

**EC.** (2 points)

**Given:** △ABC and △DEF with ∠A ≅ ∠D and ∠B ≅ ∠E
**Prove:** ∠C ≅ ∠F

**Proof:**
In △ABC: m∠A + m∠B + m∠C = 180° (Triangle Angle Sum Theorem)
In △DEF: m∠D + m∠E + m∠F = 180° (Triangle Angle Sum Theorem)

Since ∠A ≅ ∠D, m∠A = m∠D
Since ∠B ≅ ∠E, m∠B = m∠E

Substituting:
m∠A + m∠B + m∠C = m∠D + m∠E + m∠F
m∠A + m∠B + m∠C = m∠A + m∠B + m∠F

Subtracting m∠A + m∠B from both sides:
m∠C = m∠F

Therefore, ∠C ≅ ∠F ✓

**Grading:**
- 1 point for using Triangle Angle Sum Theorem
- 1 point for correct algebraic reasoning and conclusion
