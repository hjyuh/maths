**Bonus**
22. Let m = money Sarah had originally
    m - 15 = 23
    m = 38
    Sarah had $38 originally.

    Explanation: We use subtraction because Sarah spent money (subtracted from what she had), and we know what was left. To find the original amount, we use the inverse operation (addition) to solve: 23 + 15 = 38.
