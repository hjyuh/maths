## Answer Key (For Teacher Use)

### Part 1: Multiple Choice
1. C (96)
2. C (125) - V = 5³ = 125
3. B (36π) - A = πr² = π(6)² = 36π
4. C (One-third) - Cone volume = ⅓ cylinder volume
5. C (8:27) - Volume ratio = (scale factor)³ = (2/3)³ = 8/27
6. B (20π) - A = (72°/360°)π(10)² = (1/5)(100π) = 20π
7. B (100π) - SA = 4πr² = 4π(5)² = 100π
8. A (50) - A = ½h(b₁ + b₂) = ½(5)(8 + 12) = 50

### Part 2: Basic Area and Perimeter

**9.**
Area = bh = 14 × 6 = **84 cm²**
Perimeter = 2(14) + 2(8) = 28 + 16 = **44 cm**

**10.**
Area = ½bh = ½(16)(9) = **72 square units**

**11.**
a) Circumference = πd = 18π ≈ **56.55 cm**
b) Area = πr² = π(9)² = **81π ≈ 254.47 cm²**

### Part 3: Circles - Arc Length and Sectors

**12.**
Arc length = (120°/360°) · 2π(15) = (1/3) · 30π = **10π ≈ 31.42 cm**

Sector area = (120°/360°) · π(15)² = (1/3) · 225π = **75π ≈ 235.62 cm²**

**13.**
a) Central angle = 360° ÷ 6 = **60°**

b) Area of one slice = (60°/360°) · π(7)²
   = (1/6) · 49π
   = **49π/6 ≈ 25.66 in²**

### Part 4: Surface Area

**14.**
a) Surface area = 2(lw + lh + wh)
   = 2(6·9 + 6·12 + 9·12)
   = 2(54 + 72 + 108)
   = 2(234)
   = **468 square units**

b) Volume = lwh = 6 × 9 × 12 = **648 cubic units**

**15.**
SA = 2πr(r + h)
SA = 2π(7)(7 + 10)
SA = 2π(7)(17)
SA = 238π
**SA ≈ 747.70 cm²**

**16.**
a) Slant height = √(r² + h²) = √(6² + 8²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = **10 m**

b) Total SA = πr(r + l)
   = π(6)(6 + 10)
   = π(6)(16)
   = 96π
   **SA ≈ 301.59 m²**

### Part 5: Volume

**17.**
V = πr²h
V = π(5)²(12)
V = π(25)(12)
V = 300π
**V ≈ 942.48 cubic units**

**18.**
V = ⅓Bh
Base area B = 10² = 100
V = ⅓(100)(12)
V = 400
**V = 400 cubic units**

**19.**
V = (4/3)πr³
V = (4/3)π(9)³
V = (4/3)π(729)
V = 972π
**V ≈ 3,053.63 cubic units**

### Part 6: Composite Figures

**20.**
Rectangular prism volume:
V₁ = lwh = 30 × 40 × 50 = 60,000 ft³

Pyramid volume:
Base area = 30 × 40 = 1200 ft²
V₂ = ⅓Bh = ⅓(1200)(15) = 6,000 ft³

Total volume = 60,000 + 6,000 = **66,000 ft³**

### Part 7: Density and Displacement

**21.**
a) Volume = 5 × 10 × 8 = **400 cm³**

b) Mass = Density × Volume
   = 8.96 × 400
   = **3,584 grams** (or 3.584 kg)

### Part 8: Real-World Application

**22.**
a) Cylinder volume:
   V = πr²h
   V = π(12)²(35)
   V = π(144)(35)
   V = 5,040π
   **V ≈ 15,833.46 ft³**

b) Cone volume:
   V = ⅓πr²h
   V = ⅓π(12)²(8)
   V = ⅓π(144)(8)
   V = 384π
   **V ≈ 1,206.37 ft³**

c) Total volume:
   5,040π + 384π = 5,424π
   **Total ≈ 17,039.83 ft³**

### BONUS Section

**BONUS 1:**
Scale factor = 4:7
Surface area ratio = k² = (4/7)² = 16/49

Let SA₁ = 320π (smaller)
SA₁/SA₂ = 16/49
320π/SA₂ = 16/49
SA₂ = 320π × (49/16)
SA₂ = 980π

**Answer: 980π ≈ 3,078.76 cm²**

**BONUS 2:**
Cube edge = 10
Cube volume = 10³ = 1000

Sphere radius = 5 (half the edge length)
Sphere volume = (4/3)π(5)³ = (4/3)π(125) = 500π/3

Space between = 1000 - 500π/3
= 1000 - 166.67π
≈ 1000 - 523.60

**Answer ≈ 476.40 cubic units**

Or exact: **1000 - (500π/3) cubic units**
