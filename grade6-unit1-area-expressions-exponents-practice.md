## Section 1: Area of Polygons (10 problems)

**1.** Find the area of a rectangle with length 8 cm and width 5 cm.

**2.** Calculate the area of a triangle with base 12 inches and height 7 inches.

**3.** A parallelogram has a base of 15 feet and a height of 9 feet. What is its area?

**4.** Find the area of a trapezoid with bases of 10 cm and 6 cm, and a height of 8 cm.

**5.** An L-shaped room can be divided into two rectangles. One rectangle is 10 ft by 8 ft, and the other is 5 ft by 6 ft. What is the total area of the room?

**6.** A polygon can be decomposed into a rectangle (6 m × 4 m) and a triangle (base 6 m, height 3 m). Find the total area.

**7.** Find the area of a triangle with base 20 cm and height 15 cm.

**8.** A kite has diagonals of 12 inches and 16 inches. Its area can be found using the formula A = ½d₁d₂. Calculate the area.

**9.** A composite figure consists of a rectangle (8 cm × 5 cm) with a triangle on top (base 8 cm, height 4 cm). Find the total area.

**10.** A parallelogram and a rectangle have the same base (10 in) and height (7 in). What is the total area of both shapes combined?

## Section 2: Surface Area and Nets (8 problems)

**11.** A rectangular prism has dimensions: length = 6 cm, width = 4 cm, height = 3 cm. Find the surface area.

**12.** Draw a net for a cube with side length 5 inches. Calculate its surface area.

**13.** A box has length 10 in, width 8 in, and height 5 in. What is the surface area?

**14.** A rectangular prism has a square base with side length 4 cm and height 9 cm. Find the surface area.

**15.** A net consists of 2 rectangles (5 cm × 3 cm), 2 rectangles (5 cm × 2 cm), and 2 rectangles (3 cm × 2 cm). What 3D figure does this represent? What is its surface area?

**16.** True or False: A cube with side length 6 cm has a surface area of 216 cm². Explain your answer.

**17.** If a rectangular prism has dimensions that are all doubled, by what factor does the surface area increase?

**18.** A triangular prism has two triangular faces (base 6 cm, height 4 cm) and three rectangular faces (6 cm × 10 cm, 5 cm × 10 cm, and 5 cm × 10 cm). Find the total surface area.

## Section 7: Real-World Applications (12 problems)

**59.** Write an expression for the cost of buying x pencils at $0.50 each.

**60.** A rectangle has length (2w + 3) and width w. Write an expression for its perimeter.

**61.** Maria has $50. She buys lunch for $8 and then buys n notebooks at $3 each. Write an expression for the money she has left.

**62.** A garden is in the shape of a rectangle with length 15 feet and width 12 feet. Find the area of the garden.

**63.** A gift box is 10 inches long, 8 inches wide, and 4 inches tall. How much wrapping paper (surface area) is needed to cover it?

**64.** The expression 3.50h represents the cost of parking for h hours. How much does it cost to park for 5 hours?

**65.** A swimming pool is being filled with water. The expression 50t represents the number of gallons after t minutes. How many gallons are in the pool after 12 minutes?

**66.** Write an expression for the total number of legs on c cats and d dogs.

**67.** A cell phone plan costs $30 per month plus $0.10 per text message. Write an expression for the monthly cost if you send t text messages.

**68.** The area of a square is 144 square inches. What is the length of one side?

**69.** A room is 12 feet by 10 feet. Carpet costs $4 per square foot. What is the total cost to carpet the room?

**70.** A cube-shaped box has a surface area of 150 cm². What is the area of one face?
