## Section 1: Basic Theoretical Probability (10 problems)

**1.** You roll a standard die. What is P(rolling a 5)?

**2.** You flip a fair coin. What is P(heads)?

**3.** A bag contains 10 red marbles and 5 blue marbles. What is P(drawing a red marble)?

**4.** A spinner has 8 equal sections numbered 1-8. What is P(landing on 3)?

**5.** You roll a die. What is P(rolling an odd number)?

**6.** A bag has 3 red, 4 blue, and 5 green marbles. What is P(drawing green)?

**7.** You spin a spinner with 6 equal sections. What is P(landing on a number greater than 4)?

**8.** You draw a card from a standard deck. What is P(drawing a heart)?

**9.** A jar contains 8 chocolate and 12 vanilla cookies. What is P(drawing chocolate)?

**10.** You roll a die. What is P(rolling a number less than 5)?

## Section 4: Experimental Probability (10 problems)

**29.** A player makes 15 out of 20 free throws. What is the experimental P(making a free throw)?

**30.** A coin is flipped 50 times and lands on heads 28 times. What is the experimental P(heads)?

**31.** A factory tests 200 items and finds 8 defective. What is the experimental P(defective)?

**32.** It rained 12 out of 30 days in April. What is the experimental P(rain on an April day)?

**33.** A die was rolled 60 times. The number 4 came up 9 times. What is the experimental P(4)?

**34.** A student answered 42 out of 50 questions correctly. What is the experimental P(correct)?

**35.** A spinner was spun 80 times and landed on red 20 times. What is the experimental P(red)?

**36.** A basketball player made 28 out of 35 shots. What is the experimental P(making a shot)?

**37.** In 100 trials, an event occurred 35 times. What is the experimental probability?

**38.** A traffic light was red 45 out of 90 times observed. What is the experimental P(red)?

## Section 5: Using Probability to Predict (10 problems)

**39.** You flip a coin 200 times. How many heads would you expect?

**40.** You roll a die 120 times. How many times would you expect to roll a 3?

**41.** If P(success) = 0.8 and you have 50 trials, how many successes do you expect?

**42.** A machine produces defective items 5% of the time. In 1,000 items, how many defects are expected?

**43.** If P(rain) = 0.3 for 20 days, on how many days would you expect rain?

**44.** A player makes 75% of free throws. In 40 attempts, how many makes are expected?

**45.** You draw from a bag where P(red) = 2/5. In 100 draws (with replacement), how many red are expected?

**46.** If P(winning) = 1/8, how many wins would you expect in 64 games?

**47.** A spinner has P(blue) = 1/4. In 80 spins, how many times would you expect blue?

**48.** If theoretical P(heads) = 0.5 but experimental shows 60% heads in 50 flips, how many heads occurred?
