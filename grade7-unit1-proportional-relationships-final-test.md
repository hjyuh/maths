## Part 1: Ratios and Rates (20 points)

**1.** (4 points) Express the ratio 15:25 in simplest form.

**2.** (5 points) Find the unit rate: A car travels 273 miles in 6 hours.

**3.** (5 points) Which is the better buy: 4 pounds of rice for $6.20 or 7 pounds for $10.15? Show your work and explain your reasoning.

**4.** (6 points) The ratio of teachers to students at a school is 1:22. If there are 550 students, how many teachers are there?

## Part 2: Proportional Relationships (25 points)

**5.** (6 points) Determine if the relationship in this table is proportional. Explain your reasoning and, if proportional, find the constant of proportionality.
```
x | y
--|--
3 | 12
5 | 20
8 | 32
```

**6.** (5 points) Does the equation y = 6x - 2 represent a proportional relationship? Explain why or why not.

**7.** (6 points) If y is proportional to x and y = 35 when x = 7, write the equation for this relationship. Then find y when x = 12.

**8.** (8 points) The table shows a proportional relationship between hours worked and money earned.
```
Hours (h) | Earnings ($)
----------|-------------
3         | 45
5         | 75
8         | ?
```
a) Find the constant of proportionality
b) Write an equation for this relationship
c) Complete the table for 8 hours
