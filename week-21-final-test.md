### BONUS Section

**BONUS 1:**
Let Circle A have center O₁ and radius r₁
Let Circle B have center O₂ and radius r₂

Similarity transformation:
1. **Translation**: Translate circle A so that O₁ maps to O₂
2. **Dilation**: Apply dilation centered at O₂ with scale factor k = r₂/r₁

After translation, circles are concentric.
After dilation, all points at distance r₁ from O₂ map to distance kr₁ = (r₂/r₁) · r₁ = r₂ from O₂.

Therefore, Circle A maps onto Circle B, proving all circles are similar.

**BONUS 2:**
Let square have vertices on circle. The diagonal of the square equals the diameter of the circle.

Diagonal of square = side√2 = 10√2

Diameter = 10√2
Radius = (10√2)/2 = **5√2 ≈ 7.07**
