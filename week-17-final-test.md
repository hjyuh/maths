**12.** (5 points)

**Show AB ∥ DC:**

**Show AD ∥ BC:**

**Conclusion:**

**13.** (5 points)

**Classification: Square**

**Justification:**

**Step 1: Check if all sides are congruent**
PQ = |5 - 0| = 5
QR = |5 - 0| = 5
RS = |5 - 0| = 5

All sides = 5 ✓

**Step 2: Check for right angle**
Slope of PQ = 0 (horizontal)
Slope of PS = undefined (vertical)
Horizontal and vertical lines are perpendicular → ∠P = 90° ✓

**Step 3: Verify opposite sides parallel (parallelogram)**
PQ ∥ SR (both horizontal, slope = 0) ✓
PS ∥ QR (both vertical, slope undefined) ✓

**Conclusion:**
Since PQRS has all sides congruent, a right angle, and opposite sides parallel, it is a **square**. ✓
