### Part 7: Proofs

**21.**
Given: In △ABC, D is on AB, E is on AC, and DE ∥ BC

Prove: △ADE ~ △ABC

Proof:
1. DE ∥ BC (Given)
2. ∠ADE ≅ ∠ABC (Corresponding angles formed by transversal AB)
3. ∠AED ≅ ∠ACB (Corresponding angles formed by transversal AC)
4. ∠A ≅ ∠A (Reflexive Property)
5. △ADE ~ △ABC (AA Similarity Theorem)
