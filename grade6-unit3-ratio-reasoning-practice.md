# Grade 6 Unit 3: Practice Problems

## Instructions
- Show all work for each problem
- Simplify ratios when appropriate
- Check your work using the answer key at the end
- If you get a problem wrong, review the notes and try again

---

## Section 1: Writing Ratios (10 problems)

**1.** A basket contains 8 apples and 5 oranges. Write the ratio of apples to oranges in three ways.

**2.** In a parking lot, there are 12 cars and 7 trucks. Write the ratio of trucks to total vehicles as a fraction.

**3.** A paint mixture contains 3 parts red and 4 parts white. Write the ratio of red to white using a colon.

**4.** Sarah scored 18 points out of 25 on a quiz. Write the ratio of points earned to total points.

**5.** A garden has 15 roses and 10 tulips. Write the ratio of tulips to roses in simplest form.

**6.** In a class, there are 14 girls and 16 boys. Write the ratio of girls to boys in simplest form.

**7.** A recipe calls for 2 cups of flour and 1 cup of sugar. Write the ratio of flour to sugar.

**8.** A store sold 24 blue shirts and 18 red shirts. Write the ratio of blue shirts to total shirts sold.

**9.** On a team, the ratio of 6th graders to 7th graders is 5 to 3. If there are 15 sixth graders, how many 7th graders are there?

**10.** Write three different part-to-part ratios that can be formed from 6 cats, 4 dogs, and 2 birds.

## Section 2: Equivalent Ratios (12 problems)

**11.** Create three equivalent ratios for 3:5.

**12.** Create three equivalent ratios for 4:7.

**13.** Are the ratios 6:8 and 9:12 equivalent? Show your work.

**14.** Are the ratios 5:15 and 2:6 equivalent? Show your work.

**15.** Simplify the ratio 12:18.

**16.** Simplify the ratio 24:36.

**17.** Simplify the ratio 15:25.

**18.** Complete the equivalent ratio: 4:5 = 12:___

**19.** Complete the equivalent ratio: 7:3 = ___:15

**20.** Complete the equivalent ratio: 2:9 = 8:___

**21.** Which ratio is NOT equivalent to 3:4?
   a) 6:8
   b) 9:12
   c) 12:15
   d) 15:20

**22.** Write two ratios equivalent to 5:2, one using multiplication and one using division (if the ratio can be simplified).

## Section 3: Ratio Tables (10 problems)

**23.** Complete the ratio table:

| y | 5 | 10| ? | 20| ? |

**24.** Complete the ratio table:

|-------|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cost  | $6| $12| $18| ?| $30|

**25.** A car travels 120 miles in 2 hours. Create a ratio table showing the distance traveled in 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 hours.

**26.** At a store, 3 notebooks cost $9. Create a ratio table to find the cost of 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 notebooks.

**27.** A recipe uses 2 eggs for every 3 cups of milk. Create a ratio table to find how many cups of milk are needed for 6, 8, and 10 eggs.

**28.** The ratio of boys to girls is 4:3. If there are 12 boys, how many girls are there? Use a ratio table.

**29.** A machine produces 15 widgets in 3 minutes. How many widgets can it produce in 12 minutes? Use a ratio table.

**30.** Sugar and water are mixed in a ratio of 2:5. If you use 10 cups of water, how much sugar do you need? Use a ratio table.

**31.** On a map, 1 inch represents 25 miles. Create a ratio table for 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 inches.

**32.** A printer prints 8 pages per minute. Create a ratio table showing pages printed in 3, 6, 9, and 12 minutes.

## Section 4: Part-to-Part and Part-to-Whole Ratios (10 problems)

**33.** The ratio of cats to dogs at a shelter is 3:5. If there are 24 total animals, how many are cats and how many are dogs?

**34.** In a fruit basket, the ratio of apples to oranges is 4:3. If there are 21 fruits total, how many apples are there?

**35.** A paint mixture uses red and blue paint in a ratio of 2:3.
   a) What fraction of the mixture is red paint?
   b) What fraction of the mixture is blue paint?

**36.** In a class, the ratio of students who prefer pizza to those who prefer burgers is 5:2. If there are 28 students total, how many prefer pizza?

**37.** Money is divided between two people in a ratio of 3:7. If the total amount is $100, how much does each person receive?

**38.** A solution contains water and juice in a ratio of 4:1. If you make 25 cups of the solution, how many cups of each ingredient do you need?

**39.** The ratio of fiction books to non-fiction books in a library is 5:3. If there are 120 fiction books, how many non-fiction books are there?

**40.** A necklace has red and blue beads in a ratio of 3:4. If there are 35 beads total, how many are blue?

**41.** Three friends share money in the ratio 2:3:5. If they have $60 total, how much does each person receive?

**42.** In a bag of marbles, the ratio of red to blue to green is 4:3:2. If there are 36 marbles total, how many of each color are there?

## Section 5: Comparing Ratios (8 problems)

**43.** Store A sells 5 apples for $3. Store B sells 8 apples for $5. Which store has the better price per apple?

**44.** Car A travels 240 miles on 8 gallons of gas. Car B travels 300 miles on 12 gallons. Which car has better gas mileage?

**45.** Recipe A uses 3 cups of flour for 2 cups of sugar. Recipe B uses 6 cups of flour for 5 cups of sugar. Which recipe has a higher ratio of flour to sugar?

**46.** Which ratio is larger: 5:3 or 7:4?

**47.** A basketball player made 12 out of 15 free throws. Another player made 16 out of 20 free throws. Which player has a better free throw ratio?

**48.** Job A pays $120 for 8 hours of work. Job B pays $200 for 15 hours of work. Which job pays better per hour?

**49.** A 12-ounce bottle of juice costs $2.40. A 20-ounce bottle costs $3.60. Which is the better buy?

**50.** Two mixtures are made:
   - Mixture A: 3 parts concentrate to 5 parts water
   - Mixture B: 5 parts concentrate to 8 parts water
   Which mixture is stronger (has more concentrate)?

## Section 6: Real-World Ratio Problems (12 problems)

**51.** A recipe for 4 servings calls for 2 cups of rice. How much rice is needed for 10 servings?

**52.** If 6 pencils cost $3, how much do 15 pencils cost?

**53.** A car travels 180 miles in 3 hours. At this rate, how far can it travel in 5 hours?

**54.** A photocopier can make 80 copies in 2 minutes. How many copies can it make in 7 minutes?

**55.** On a map, 2 inches represents 50 miles. How many miles do 7 inches represent?

**56.** If 4 pounds of bananas cost $2.80, what is the cost of 7 pounds?

**57.** A machine fills 120 bottles in 5 minutes. How long will it take to fill 360 bottles?

**58.** The scale on a blueprint is 1 inch = 8 feet. If a room is 2.5 inches long on the blueprint, how long is the actual room?

**59.** A train travels 240 kilometers in 3 hours. How many kilometers does it travel in 45 minutes?

**60.** If 3 workers can paint a fence in 6 hours, how long would it take 9 workers? (Assume they work at the same rate)

**61.** A car uses 12 gallons of gas to travel 360 miles. How many gallons are needed to travel 450 miles?

**62.** A store offers a deal: Buy 3 shirts and get 2 free. If you want 15 shirts total, how many do you need to buy?

---

## Section 7: Challenge Problems (8 problems)

**63.** The ratio of the length to width of a rectangle is 5:3. If the perimeter is 64 cm, find the length and width.

**64.** A pet store has birds, cats, and dogs in a ratio of 4:3:2. If there are 18 cats, how many pets are in the store total?

**65.** A fruit punch recipe calls for cranberry juice, apple juice, and sparkling water in a ratio of 3:2:4. If you want to make 36 cups of punch, how much of each ingredient do you need?

**66.** In a school, the ratio of teachers to students is 1:15. If there are 12 teachers, how many students are there?

**67.** A bag contains red, blue, and green marbles in the ratio 5:3:2. If you add 10 more red marbles, the new ratio of red to blue becomes 3:1. How many marbles of each color were in the bag originally?

**68.** The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2:3:4. Find the measure of each angle. (Hint: The sum of angles in a triangle is 180°)

**69.** Two numbers are in the ratio 3:5. If their sum is 56, find the two numbers.

**70.** A rope is cut into three pieces in the ratio 2:3:5. If the rope was 60 feet long, how long is each piece?
